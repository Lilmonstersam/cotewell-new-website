Our story

Built on a century of getting the details right.

Cotewell began when Thomas Hiscock Snr established a painting, glazing and signwriting business in 1894. Each generation kept the same practical mindset while moving the business towards industrial flooring, line marking and visual safety.

Today, the fourth and fifth generations work together to inspire team excellence for industrial workplaces.

Our history

Five generations
of progress.

Family knowledge, carried forward and sharpened for modern industrial sites.

  1. 1894

    The beginning

    Thomas Hiscock Snr establishes T. Hiscock & Sons Painting Company.

  2. 1907

    Second generation

    Tom Hiscock takes over and leads the business for 40 years.

  3. 1965

    A new chapter

    Brian Hiscock joins, then takes over management the following year.

  4. 1984

    Industrial focus

    Nelson joins Brian and drives a stronger focus on innovation.

  5. 1998

    Cotewell

    The Cotewell name emerges from the purpose-built head office and warehouse.

  6. 2011 to today

    Fifth generation

    Nelson's sons join the family business and build its national reach.
