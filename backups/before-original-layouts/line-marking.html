External markings

Cold Applied
Plastic (CAP).

Where heavy vehicles turn on the line, or the surface is too rough for tape, ask about Cold Applied Plastic.

CAP is a thick, durable line marking system that bonds directly to the surface and cures into a hard-wearing layer. It is built for abrasion, chemical exposure and heavy vehicle traffic.
